📍 Places to see and do

Around Rethymno (close to the hotel)
  • Rethymno Old Town – Venetian streets, Ottoman minarets and lots of tavernas
  • Fortezza – the big Venetian fortress above the town, with views over the coast
  • Venetian harbour and lighthouse – sunset stroll and drinks on the quay
  • Arkadi Monastery – historic monastery in the hills inland
  • Margarites – pottery village with workshops and cafes
  • Melidoni Cave – large cave with a moving history, a short drive east
Further afield (day trips)
  • Chania Old Town – about an hour west; harbour, lighthouse and old town
  • Knossos and Heraklion – about 1h15 east; the Minoan palace and the archaeological museum
  • Preveli beach and palm gorge – south coast, roughly an hour away
  • Georgioupoli and Lake Kournas – long sandy beach village, and Crete's only freshwater lake nearby
  • Spili – mountain village known for its lion-head fountains
  • Seitan Limania – small dramatic cove near Chania
Live music
  • Rethymno Old Town – the harbour front and nearby streets have bars and tavernas with live music in the evenings. Check venues' social pages for that week's lineup.
  • Fagotto Jazz Bar, Chania – long-running live jazz and blues venue, good if we spend a day in Chania.

Opening times and lineups change, so confirm before heading out. Elafonisi and Balos are well over 2 hours each way from the hotel, so I have left them off.
